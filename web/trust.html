Trust & safety

What ArchiveCheck does — and what it will never do.

What this tool does

ArchiveCheck searches public archived posts — posts that were published in public and captured by a web archive (the Wayback Machine, Arquivo.pt, Common Crawl, multi-archive TimeMaps) or that are still live on a public feed (Bluesky, Mastodon, YouTube). A match proves a public post existed on the date it was captured. Nothing more.

Found ≠ guilty. Not found ≠ not real.

A found post does not prove who wrote it, why it was deleted, or what happened off the public record. A post that isn't found may never have been archived, may have been posted under a different handle, or may simply not be saved anywhere yet.

What it never does

  • No private messages. We cannot read DMs, private accounts, or anything behind a login — and we never ask for your login or anyone else's.
  • No account access. No passwords, no sessions, no "log in as" anything. Searches use public archive data and public APIs only.
  • No location tracking. We don't track where anyone is, has been, or lives.
  • No real-time monitoring or alerts. We don't watch accounts, follow people, or notify you when someone posts. Every search is a one-off lookup you run yourself.
  • No face recognition or photo search. The archives hold text and links, not biometric matching. Reverse-image-check a profile photo separately (Google Lens / TinEye) — that is outside this tool.
  • No verdicts or scores. There is no "trust score", no "catfish probability", no guilty/not-guilty label. You see dated captures and decide for yourself.
  • No minors. Do not use this tool to look up anyone under 18.
  • No harassment, stalking, or confrontation. Do not use results to harass, stalk, threaten, or ambush someone. If you are checking a dating profile, check your own safety first — and if you feel unsafe, contact a local support helpline before anything else.

FCRA disclaimer — not a consumer report

ArchiveCheck is not a consumer reporting agency and its results are not consumer reports under the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) or any equivalent law.

Do not use ArchiveCheck for employment screening, tenant screening, credit decisions, insurance underwriting, or any other decision covered by the FCRA. Hiring use requires a compliant screening provider and the candidate's consent where the law requires it — an archive search is not a background check. Do not use protected characteristics (age, health, religion, race, and similar) found in archives in any decision.

For legal use — Triage, not court-ready

Evidence packs preserve a dated, citable record: archived snapshot URL, capture timestamp, and digest, with a SHA-256 manifest. That is Triage, not court-ready evidence. Verify every snapshot yourself at web.archive.org before relying on it in a filing, and have counsel confirm what your jurisdiction requires.

Privacy — local-first

  • Searches run in your browser. Your licence key is stored on your device only and is never sent anywhere (demo keys are checked locally; real keys are verified server-side after purchase).
  • We never log searched handles, search results, or licence keys. Your search history and usage counts live in your browser's local storage.
  • Analytics is page views only, via Cloudflare Web Analytics (free, cookieless) when enabled. Sales come from our payment provider (Paddle).
  • Evidence packs you save are yours. Paid packs are stored for 12 months (free: 30 days), with a 30-day grace period after a refund, and deleted immediately on request.

Report abuse / request a takedown

If this tool is being used to harass or stalk someone, or if you want a result about you removed from our pages, email sales@example.com with the archived link and what happened. We review reports by hand and act on them.

We cannot delete posts from the Wayback Machine or any other archive — those are run by their own operators. We can remove our own pages and links that point at them.
