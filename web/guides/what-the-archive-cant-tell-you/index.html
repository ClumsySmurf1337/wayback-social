What the archive can't tell you

The limits page. Every search on this site is honest about what it found — this page is honest about everything it didn't, and why.

Coverage is uneven — by network and by archive

CoverageNetworksWhat that means
GoodX, Reddit, Bluesky, MastodonPost URLs carry the handle, captures are plentiful, live checks work.
ModerateInstagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Threads, Tumblr, TwitchCaptures exist but are patchy; some replay as blank shells; live checks are limited or login-walled.
PoorLinkedIn, Pinterest, MediumThin archives; a search here usually returns little, and that is the archive, not the search failing.

Coverage is also uneven by archive: Arquivo.pt is Portuguese-web centred with a ≥1-year embargo; Ghostarchive is a small volunteer project with no SLA; Common Crawl is monthly and URL-indexed, not a keyword search; archive.today only holds what a human chose to save. The console labels every network and every source rather than pretending the coverage is uniform.

Not found ≠ not real

A post that isn't found may never have been archived, may have been posted under a different handle, may live under a URL that doesn't contain the username (YouTube watch URLs, Instagram /p/ codes, Pinterest pins), or may simply not be saved anywhere yet. Absence from the archive means "not captured" — not "never posted" and not "innocent". The reverse is just as important: found ≠ guilty. A found post does not prove who wrote it, why it was deleted, or what anyone intended.

No full-text search in the Wayback Machine

CDX — the index this tool queries — returns URLs and dates, not words. It can filter on the URL, not on the text inside a post. Keyword search here works by fetching the archived replays and searching your own downloaded copy, capped by tier (Pro: 1,000 text fetches per search) — and it is slow, rate-limited, and often returns blank JavaScript shells for Instagram, TikTok and X. Arquivo.pt is the one archive with real full-text search, and it is the Pro-tier fallback, labelled "≥1 year old, Portuguese-web weighted". Anyone selling "search all of X for a word" from CDX alone is selling something that does not exist.

Blank shells, placeholder IDs, and a flaky archive

  • Blank shells: many captures replay as an empty page — the post text may still be in the page source, or recoverable through oEmbed. A blank replay proves the URL was captured on that date; it does not prove the content.
  • Placeholder IDs: sequential IDs (1, 10, 1000) answer 404 but were never real posts. The diff skips listing rows with no post ID so a fake "deleted" verdict can't be manufactured from them.
  • A flaky archive: archive.org answers 503, stalls, and goes down for ~20 minutes at a time. A failed search is reported as a failure with a retry — never silently shown as "no captures found".
  • Captures disappear: owners can request exclusions and removals, and the service can throttle or go down. Never promise a capture exists.

Identity, intent, and the things no archive holds

  • Same handle ≠ same person. CDX lists captures, not authorship. False positives are routine — a handle can be reused, renamed, or impersonated.
  • No private anything. DMs, private accounts, and anything behind a login were never captured and are never shown. Stories are almost never archived.
  • No images, faces, or locations. CDX digests are hashes of whole captures, not perceptual hashes — there is no image index and no face index. The tool links out to Google Lens / TinEye for same-image checks and refuses face search outright.
  • No verdicts. There is no trust score, no "catfish probability", no guilty/not-guilty label. You see dated captures and decide for yourself.

Why this page exists. Competitors over-promise; labelling builds the trust a $19 one-time tool needs. If a result looks too clean or too damning, re-read this page before you act on it — and verify every snapshot yourself at web.archive.org.

Honest limits

Public archived posts only. Found ≠ guilty. Not found ≠ not real.

Never use results to harass, stalk, or confront anyone; never look up anyone under 18; never use archive results for employment, tenant, credit, or insurance decisions — this is not a consumer report. Trust & safety
