Honest limits

Public archived posts only. Found ≠ guilty. Not found ≠ not real.

A found post does not prove who wrote it, why it was deleted, or what anyone intended. A post that isn't found may never have been archived. Never use results to harass, stalk, or confront anyone — and never look up anyone under 18. Trust & safety
