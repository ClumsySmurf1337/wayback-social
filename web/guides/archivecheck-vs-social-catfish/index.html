ArchiveCheck vs Social Catfish

One is a $27.48-a-month people search. The other is $19 once for an archived-post timeline. They answer different questions — here's which question each one answers.

The price, honestly

Social Catfish's Social Search is a 3-day trial at $5.73, then $27.48 a month (100 searches a month). Its Image Search is $6.87 for 3 days, then $28.97 a month. The first month of Social Search costs $33.21; a year costs $329.76 — and the trial auto-renews, which is the part people forget to cancel.

ArchiveCheck's Pro pack is $19 once at launch ($29 regular) — 43% cheaper than Social Catfish's first month, 94% cheaper than a year of it — and there is nothing to cancel, because there is no subscription. The Starter pack is $9 once at launch ($19 regular), 73% cheaper than that first month. Launch pricing ends after 90 days or 500 buyers; after that the regular prices apply. Competitor prices were checked September–November 2026 and vary by checkout path — re-check before you rely on them.

What Social Catfish does that ArchiveCheck doesn't

  • Live people-search records — name, email, phone, username and address matching across public records and social profiles.
  • A dating-site index — whether a profile appears on dating sites.
  • Reverse-image search at scale — a 1-billion-image database, plus retained deleted profiles.
  • Human reports — a Search Specialist report from $297 for a human investigation.

None of that is in this tool, and none of it is planned. If you need a phone number or a dating-site membership, this is the wrong product.

What ArchiveCheck does that Social Catfish doesn't

  • An archived-post timeline with dates — what the handle posted in 2021, 2022, 2023, and what it deleted. Social Catfish has no deleted-post history and no cross-network archive timeline.
  • Deleted-only diff — every archived post checked live and marked live / deleted / unknown, across 14 networks.
  • One-time price, kept forever — buy once, no renewal, no trial that converts.
  • No face search, by design — reverse-image checks are free outbound links (Google Lens, TinEye). Face recognition is biometric surveillance and this tool refuses it; Social Catfish's image search is same-image matching, not face search either.

Both tools disclaim the same thing. Neither is a consumer reporting agency and neither result is a consumer report. Do not use either for employment, tenant, credit, or insurance decisions — that is FCRA territory and needs a compliant screener. And neither tool proves who a person is: a handle is not an identity, and a match is not a verdict. See Trust & safety.
